# ADR-009 — ZERO EXTRA policy and volatile manual category

**Accepted, Sprint 10 / issue #23, 2026-10-22.** Extends ADR-007/008. No production authority, permission or retention change.

The owner's non-negotiable policy is no extra payment and no Ultra Lux. We implement one pure, deterministic fictional matcher, with separate category, designation, physical topology, fictional availability and separately sourced per-role post-pass additional-due types. Color and ticket legend prices carry no eligibility/fee authority. A known Ultra Lux node is excluded before ranking and rendering even when its fabricated due is zero. Known eligible pairs are FICTIONAL_GEOMETRY/FICTIONAL_ZERO_EXTRA only. No code converts this result to a manual acknowledgement, external observation or booking.

Additional-due evidence binds role, exact seat ID, screening, revision, time and known-current-charge coverage. It cannot be copied from another seat/showing or substituted with legend/partial/unknown zero. Snapshot/fee freshness is at most 30 seconds; missing topology, aisle, gap, ambiguity or missing conditions fails closed. Explicit accessibility-aware demo opt-in plus fixture-known conditions permits appropriate fictional exploration; no health/disability details or genuine eligibility inference.

The existing manual track adds optional `MANUAL_DETAILS.category` and memory-only `track.category` (default UNKNOWN), with the existing WORKFLOW generation/revision/B-epoch guards. No extra command, worker authority or wizard. Saving own input invalidates shared preflight and clears only that role's earlier checks. Known Ultra Lux blocks preflight/acknowledgement in the authoritative manual core; other category labels never prove zero fee. Category is optional even in SOLO: the independent manual checklist remains useful while all real costs remain UNKNOWN. Unknown data is not a policy-compliant recommendation. The existing SEATS step carries the human no-extra-payment instruction; no separate fee checkbox/status or stored financial amount is introduced.

B reset/buddy change/mode and new plan/worker/reset clear the relevant category; B-only changes preserve A. The renderer includes dirty category in existing form comparisons and discards it at passive worker replacement, even when raw dirty A seat/film fields may remain as concept. Accepted explicit clears continue to discard local drafts (S08). Roster and film-concept schemas/keys/TTLs remain identical; category, fee data and accessibility preferences never enter retention, popup projections or clipboard diagnostics. The native test checks actual unchanged Chrome storage after both lab and manual category use.

The fixture-only lab is co-located under the existing read-only lab, without a new navigation hierarchy or production export. All artwork/data are invented; owner screenshots are not imported. The manual notice expresses **SeatBuddies product policy**, not verified Vue universal pricing. G0/terms remain blocked/inconclusive. A future lawful read-only integration needs its own scoped source/permission review and authorization; it is not implemented or enabled by this change.
